How training works

No training. The rules below (detect.find_barlines) run on each straightened staff, after the clef and key signature. Their constants were tuned by grid search against the editor's corrections on KHM 602 pages 2, 3 and 6 (tools/tune_barlines.py), then re-tuned on KHM 602 and 603. They know nothing of other hands: every number is in staff spaces, but the ink cutoff is a fixed grey level.

The rules, in order

  1. Ink. A pixel is ink if darker than 120 (of 255). Fixed: the faint brown ink of the Paris copy's second hand is mostly lighter, which is why the rules miss two thirds of its bar lines.
  2. Stroke coverage. For every column, try straight strokes leaning from −0.3 to +0.3 (horizontal pixels per vertical pixel, in steps of 0.03) and keep the lean whose stroke crosses the most ink between the top and bottom staff lines. Coverage is that share of the staff's height.
  3. Skip the clef and key. Columns before the music start are ignored.
  4. Candidate strokes. Runs of neighbouring columns (gaps of up to 3 pixels) whose coverage is over COVER of the staff's height. A run wider than 0.6 staff spaces is a beam or a smudge, not a line.
  5. Not a stem. Walk along the stroke from a space above the staff to a space below, skipping the staff lines' rows, and measure the ink running across it. A note head or a beam is wider than ATTACH_WIDTH spaces; if more than ATTACH_ROWS spaces' worth of rows are that wide, the stroke is a stem.
  6. Double bars. Strokes closer than 1.2 staff spaces are one double bar (or the thick and thin lines of a repeat).
  7. Bars are wide. No two bar lines closer than MIN_BAR staff spaces: of a tighter cluster, keep the stroke with the least ink across it.
  8. Faint bar lines. Bars on a line are roughly even. Where a gap is more than WIDE_GAP times the line's typical bar, accept a weaker stroke (coverage over FAINT) at least MIN_BAR spaces from either end, the strongest near the middle of the gap.
  9. The line's end. The search runs a staff space past the ruled lines' end, where a final bar line often sits.

Constants (detect.py, now)

COVER = 0.88
share of the staff's height a bar line must cover
ATTACH_WIDTH = 0.85
ink across the stroke this wide (spaces) is a note head or beam
ATTACH_ROWS = 0.25
this many spaces' worth of such rows make it a stem
MIN_BAR = 4.0
narrowest bar (staff spaces)
WIDE_GAP = 1.6
a gap this many times the typical bar may hide a faint bar line
FAINT = 0.7
coverage accepted in such a gap
LEAN = 0.3
steepest lean tried (dx per dy)
END_SLACK = 0.0
staff spaces a bar line may stop short of the outer lines
