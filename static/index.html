    Page

    Selection

    Click a staff, bar line or mark.

    Keys

    hold b + click
    add a bar line (also: double-click a staff, or ⇧-click)
    hold s + click
    add a staff
    hold m + drag
    add a mark around what you drag over
    hold c + drag
    box a clef, key or time change written mid-line; it holds through later staves until the next (at a line's start, set them on the staff)
    a
    snap the selected bar line to the ink (placing or dragging one snaps it)
    t / g + ↑ ↓
    hold t (top) or g (the key below it: bottom) and press up/down to move the selected staff's crop edge, or a selected bar's own crop; ¼ space a press, ⇧ 1
    d / Del
    delete selection
    ← →
    nudge (⇧ ×10); ⌥ moves only the top end of a bar line
    ↑ ↓
    nudge staff or mark
    click a handle
    highlight it: arrows then move just that point (⇧ ×10; crop by ¼ space, ⇧ 1); click again or Esc to let go
    Tab
    next item of the kind selected: bar line, staff or mark (⇧ previous); past a page's last, on to the next page's first
    1–6
    bar line kind: single, double, repeat start, repeat end, both, final
    1–4
    with a signature mark selected: its clef, treble, alto, tenor, bass
    e or .
    with a bar line selected: toggle “movement ends here” (. otherwise: next page)
    0
    with a bar line selected: toggle “upbeat” (bar count 0: a pickup, or the rest of a split bar)
    ]
    with a staff selected: end it at its last bar line
    hold h
    hide everything drawn over the page, to see what's under it
    z
    detail view: exactly what the selected bar, staff, mark or page covers
    Enter
    mark reviewed, next page
    , .
    previous / next page
    f / w
    fit page / fit width
    + −
    zoom; pinch or ⌃-scroll also zooms
    ⌘Z ⇧⌘Z
    undo / redo
    Esc
    deselect

    Drag empty space or scroll to pan. Drag items or their handles to adjust.
