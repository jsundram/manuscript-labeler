Pairing recheck

Each card is one staff and one question: does this staff hold exactly these bars, in this order? Each box is one measure as the reader split it. I 54 → means the staff ends partway through bar 54. I 53–54 means the reader read bars 53 and 54 as one measure.

  • Gained staves were left out before and are kept now. "Yes" means the new rules were right to keep it.
  • Lost staves were kept before and are left out now. "Yes" means the new rules threw away a good staff.

"Open in the labeler" goes to the page. It needs the labeler running on port 8050, with --archive ~/Dropbox/Code/boccherini-sources for archive sources. The labeler shows the new pairing.
