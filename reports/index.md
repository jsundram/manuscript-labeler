# Report index

Newest first. **Kind:**
- *write-up*: what we tried and what it showed;
- *page*: an interactive page, published as an artifact and saved here;
- *model card*: a released reader, how it was trained and how it scores;
- *notes*: running notes and plans.

How reports are added: [README.md](README.md). The story behind each one:
[journal.md](journal.md).

Earlier write-ups are linked where they live in the repo. A page's saved
copy is its `index.html`; open it in a browser.
