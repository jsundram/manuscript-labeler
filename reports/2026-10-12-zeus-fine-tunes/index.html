How the runs are scored

Measure
Exact bars: the share of all bars on the test staves whose notes Zeus read exactly as the edition's encoding has them (compare.py). Bars on a staff Zeus couldn't pair count as wrong.
Pairing
Zeus's measures are paired with the labelled bar boxes using only the time signature, so an extra or missed bar line, a bar split by a repeat, a multi-bar rest or a runaway doesn't lose the rest of the staff.
KHM 602 II
D-B KHM 602, Op. 48 no. 1 (G.226), movement II: 32 staves, 284 bars. The Berlin copyist. Movement I is trained on from run D on, so this measures a trained hand on music it hasn't seen.
Vma ms 1067 (1) II
F-Pn Vma ms 1067 (1), Op. 48 no. 3 (G.228), movement II: 47 staves, 268 bars. A hand no run trained on. Only movement II's encoding is trusted, so this copyist has no training staves yet. From the sweep on it is also the dev set: read during training for monitoring only, never trained on or used to pick an epoch.
RES 507 (14) II
F-Po RES 507 (14), also Op. 48 no. 3, movement II: 268 bars. Unseen in the sweep; trained on from run D on.
Noise
About ±3 points. Each test is under 300 bars, so differences smaller than that can't be told apart.
Runaways
Readings that run to Zeus's 700-token limit. Counted at 690 tokens or more.
Not used
Zeus's symbol error rate on our staves: our training files have no stems, so it improves by matching that style rather than by reading better.

The runs, one by one

Run C hurt unseen hands

Zeus's default learning rate (1e-4) for 20 epochs on KHM 602 I and II, 192 Op. 33 Paris staves and 600 Dolores staves. On hands it hadn't trained on, exact bars fell: RES 507 (14) II 79% → 76%, Vma 1067 (1) II 55% → 44%. Most of the drop came in epoch 1, and the later epochs were noisy (epoch 6: 50% and 32%), so stopping early wouldn't have saved it. Dolores test SER rose from 12% to 15% and its runaways doubled. The model was forgetting what it knew.

The sweep recipe adopted

Run C's data, 8 epochs, three ways. lr 3e-5 still cost the unseen hands a few points. lr 1e-5 cost nothing: RES 507 (14) II 77% and Vma 55–56%, the untouched model's level within noise. Zeus's full augmentation (rotation, vertical shift, noise, contrast, blur) at lr 3e-5 didn't help either. From here on: lr 1e-5, cosine decay, 8 epochs.

Run D superseded

The recipe with KHM 602 II held out, to check it still teaches a hand. KHM 602 II 45% → 62–64%, the same as the per-hand fine-tune; Vma unchanged at 55–56%. So the gain on a trained hand comes without the loss on new ones. Dolores test: SER 12.2% → 12.7% on readings that didn't run away; runaways 8 → 22 of 400.

Run E superseded

The first run with the Philhar parts: 1,713 staves paired automatically with Philhar's encodings of Op. 2, Op. 22 (Berlin and Paris), Op. 32 and Op. 33 (Paris), plus 2,000 Dolores staves. KHM 602 II rose to 78–80%. Vma rose to 60–62%, the first gain on a hand the model never saw. 476 steps an epoch, about 2 hours 20 minutes with scoring.

Run F superseded

E without Dolores. A tie overall (mean of both tests, epochs 6–8: E 70.2, F 70.3): Dolores was worth 2–4 points on KHM and cost 3 on Vma, both about the noise. F reached 62% on Vma by epoch 3. Half the data, twice as fast. Its epoch-8 model re-paired every source (below).

Run G current best

F's recipe on the staves kept by any of three detections (filter only, with the YOLO vote, with the retrained YOLOs): 2,246 Philhar staves, 30% more than F. KHM 602 II 81% (high of 85% at epoch 4, within noise). Vma flat at 64–65%. Learned faster: 79% and 66% by epoch 2. More of the same hands helped the Berlin hand and did nothing for the unseen one.

Training data

Where the Boccherini staves come from

Philhar's LilyPond encodings (boccherini-quartet-data) are trusted transcriptions of quartets we have manuscript parts for. No one labelled those manuscripts, so each staff is paired with its bars automatically:

  1. Detection finds the staves and bar lines (the labeler's own detection: rule-based staff tracing, the learned bar-line filter, and the YOLO bar-line detectors' vote).
  2. Zeus reads each staff.
  3. The reading is matched to the encoding, measure by measure, using durations. A staff is kept when it matches.
  4. Only "plain" staves go into training: one voice, every bar filled, one movement.

On the three labelled Op. 48 copies, where the answer is known, pairing with run F's model kept 103, 88 and 123 staves and got none wrong.

Scale against Zeus's own training

The untouched model trained on about 43,000 staves an epoch for 200 epochs: 10,899 scanned (Dolores 7,852, OLiMPiC scanned 2,352, OmniOMR 695) and about 32,000 rendered. Run G's 2,340 staves for 8 epochs is a nudge, which is why a gentle learning rate matters.

Why
The kept staves are the ones detection already got right, so faint or unusual bar lines never reach training, and the Philhar hands outnumber Op. 48's six to one. The detectors got better at the hands they already knew and worse at a new one.

Turning on the existing detectors' vote, with no training, gained more: Op. 32 went from 268 to 410 staves.

What these runs suggest

  • Train gently. lr 1e-5, cosine decay, 8 epochs. Higher rates and longer runs made unseen hands worse. Scores level off by epoch 4–6, at about 30% of the peak learning rate, so more epochs are unlikely to help.
  • A hand improves most from its own staves. KHM 602 went 45% → 62% from 65 staves of its own movement I, and → 81% with Op. 22 Berlin and Op. 32, which may share its copyist.
  • Breadth helps an unseen hand, up to a point. D → F added five or more new hands and gained 9 points on Vma. F → G added more staves in the same hands and gained none.
  • Dolores didn't help. See below.
  • Training on your own output narrows you. Every automatically kept staff is one the pipeline already handled, so self-training sharpens the hands it knows and can't teach what it misses. That held for the bar-line detectors and is likely to hold for Zeus.
  • Use what's already there first. The biggest detection gain came from turning on detectors that existed but weren't running for these sources.

Why Dolores was dead weight

  • The untouched model already trained on Dolores's training and validation splits, and had nearly memorized them (3.4% symbol error on validation). Showing it those staves again adds almost nothing new.
  • In a fine-tune, old data usually serves as rehearsal, to stop the model forgetting. At lr 1e-5 for 8 epochs the model barely drifts (run D's Dolores test error moved 12.2% → 12.7%), so there was little to protect.
  • Not tested: whether rehearsal would matter under a harsher recipe, such as run C's.

When to train again

  • Retrain Zeus when there's a trusted encoding in a hand it hasn't trained on. The next one is Op. 48 no. 3 movement I: it would give the Vma ms 1067 (1) copyist training staves, with movement II still the test. By analogy with KHM 602 (65 staves, +17 points), expect a large gain on that hand.
  • Don't retrain for more staves in hands already covered, for more epochs, or for Dolores.
  • For the detectors, label pages in new hands. Pseudo-labels from the pipeline don't teach it what it misses.
  • Fold in the small additions with the next real run, rather than running for them alone: the ⚠ sources (about 190 staves, three more hands) and, once dataset.py supports them, the roughly 550 staves left out as not plain (double stops, two voices, repeat-split bars).
  • Keep the tests fixed. Same three movements and the same scoring, so new runs compare with these. A third unseen hand would make the unseen-hand number less noisy.

Data not yet used

OSSQ-OMR
A string-quartet OMR benchmark (MALerLab, github.com/MALerLab/string-quartet-omr-benchmark): 116 quartet scores by 47 composers, 1773–1994. Six are handwritten manuscripts, all by women composers. None of it is in our training data, and as far as we know none was in the untouched model's. The manuscripts are full scores in later hands than our copyists', so they'd add breadth rather than resemblance.
Op. 24 autograph
F-Pn MS 1292: a full score in a hand unlike the others. Left out.
Engravings
Prints (Hummel's and others) are left out for now.
